Our story

By God’s faithful hand

Born and raised on her family’s farm in South Georgia — where she sources her homemade beef tallow — Sarah Beth Boyett founded Better Than Gold in light of a season of unexpected personal trials that God used as fertilizer to display His power, purpose, and kindness.

Better Than Gold Tallow Co. exists to translate God’s faithful handiwork into reality through soothing balms, scrubs, and other handmade self-care items to enhance your everyday.
