Our story

From the farm, for your everyday

Founded by Sarah Beth Boyett, Better Than Gold Tallow Co. exists to translate God’s faithful handiwork into reality through soothing balms, scrubs and other handmade self care items to enhance your everyday.

Born and raised on her family’s farm in South Georgia (where she sources her homemade beef tallow), Sarah Beth founded BTG in light of a season of unexpected personal trials that God used as fertilizer to display His power, purpose and kindness.
